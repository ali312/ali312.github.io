How it works

A tiny loop for better photos.

PicMatic turns one self timer into a full mini photoshoot. Move, reset, laugh, try another pose, and keep the frame alive.

01

Set the frame

Use a tripod, table, ledge, or phone stand. Compose once, then step away.

Choose the rhythm between shots and use optional flash cues before each capture.

## Design Context

### Users
Fellow makers: designers, engineers, and curious peers exploring Anton's skills and side projects. The main action is exploring projects. App users can discover the maker and his other apps.

### Brand Personality
Concise, curious, crafted. Punchy and factual, with minimal filler. Anton CGI is the primary identity; Anton Kuznetsov appears in the about section. Engineering first, with a designer's eye. No hiring or sales messaging.

### Aesthetic Direction
Concise content with a bold identity and generous surrounding whitespace. Athos informs the prominent Anton CGI name with one concise subtitle, editorial section typography, a unified Skills & tools section with substantial chips and scrolling tool icons, and overlapping project cards. Midnight-charcoal/asphalt backgrounds, cool off-white, blue-gray text, dawn-peach accents, local Satoshi, and distinct project imagery. Alpine Dawn from ascii.rest fills the viewport behind the page; the centered content has a nearly opaque midnight-charcoal backdrop, with clear sides revealing the scene. Fit the full scene without stretching, sample at 400×200 characters, and use perceptible cloud and water motion with reduced-motion support. Anton explicitly dislikes green UI styling; do not use it for accents or project decoration. Avoid filler copy, sprawling sections, generic service-site layouts, invented metrics, and decorative card grids.

### Design Principles
- Lead with engineering and demonstrate craft through shipped side projects.
- Give Polara and PicMatic full-width cards that overlap on scroll, with top-aligned text beside artwork on desktop and text above artwork on mobile. Use Polara.html's actual three-slide canvas strip for its preview. Keep each icon, title, and description directly on the same rounded, borderless card surface, without a nested text panel. Preserve the minimal controls and divider-free layout.
- Make every word useful and every project destination easy to find.
- Recompose for mobile; preserve keyboard access, reduced motion, and content without JavaScript.
- Isolate homepage styles from app pages and keep assets local and appropriately sized.
